# Results used in the paper

Each file here is written by `analysis/paper_results.py` from the pooled tables of the experiments over seeds and the run folders behind them, or from single runs where an experiment is one run. The tables under `tables/` are written first and every figure under `figures/` is drawn from them, so `python analysis/paper_results.py` reproduces the folder from the pulled runs, and `tables/settings.csv` holds the ε, δ, π_max and π_cal of every experiment with its first and last run id. The table below repeats those runs with the source runs of the experiments that read another one, and is updated with the folder.

The runs of `e11`, `e12`, `e13` and `e13_ugr16_floor` above repeat the runs before them to the bit on every table, histogram and saved draw of the methods those held, among them the `e13` runs `e24` reads, and add the three published baselines named below.

The runs of `e21`, `e23` and `e24` hold two streams, the simulated one and the replay of the real order, in a `stream` column of their tables; the paper's figures and the E7 monitoring stage read the replay, the simulated stream stays beside it as the check. The runs of `e11`, `e12` and `e13` hold LTT at an estimated prevalence in a plug in and an upper bound variant, `ltt_plugin` and `ltt_upper`, beside the other methods, and since the batch of 2026-10-22 the three baselines of the related work at their published settings: the conformal FNR of Şahin and Mert at their β = 0.01 (`sahin_mert`), the Neyman-Pearson umbrella of Tong, Feng and Li at the same level with δ = 0.05 (`np_umbrella`), and the conformal selection of Jin and Candès on every window at level ε (`conformal_selection`). They appear in the tables only, the figures keeping their eight methods, and the E1 tables carry for every method the mean share of attacks among the dismissed flows of a window (`realized_for_mean`) and the mean miss rate at the window's mix (`miss_mean`). UGR16 runs at ε = 0.005 with π_max = 0.1, its operating target, and its certificate starts at half of the benign flows, the share start, where AnoShift and LITNET keep the feasible start; the fixed threshold and conformal FNR of `e13` start at the same share, where the certificate and LTT start, and the recalibration of `e24` starts from that fixed threshold. `e13_ugr16_floor` is `e13` at the paper's ε = 0.001, where the certificate refuses most calibration sets; its rows join the E1 tables and the settings with their ε, and the E1 figure keeps the operating target. `tables/settings.csv` carries the settings of every experiment.

Every pooled share of the E3, E4 and E6 tables is pooled over its own units, the draws, and the draws with an alarm for the share of alarms above the bound of Theorem 3; a seed without the share adds nothing, and a share no seed has stays empty. The tables over seeds read the run folders behind the pooled tables, every seed judging its methods and arms on the same calibration draws and windows, so a difference between two methods or two arms is paired within a seed and the seeds are independent. A difference comes with its median, its 5% to 95% range and its extremes over seeds, the seeds above, at and below zero, and the exact two-sided sign test, with the p values of a table adjusted together by Holm (`_p_holm`). The pooled shares of `e1_for_by_prevalence.csv` and `e3_by_policy.csv` carry two-sided 95% Clopper-Pearson intervals (`_ci_low`, `_ci_high`) taking the draws of every seed as the units, the sequences of every draw for the share of sequences leaving the bounds and the alarms for the share of alarms above the bound; the windows of one draw share its calibration set, so the E1 intervals count draws rather than draw and window pairs. `e7_contract.csv` certifies every pair of ε and π_max again on the calibration sets of every `e11`, `e12` and `e13` run, with the run's δ, family groups and start rule. Both it and `e1_start_rules.csv`, which reads the `e13` runs, redraw the calibration sets with the numpy of the machine that writes them, the runs' own draws where that numpy matches Colab's version and draws of the same law elsewhere.

The checks of E0 read one run each of `e10`, `e14` and `e20`, run again on 2026-10-23 at `de8e0d8`, after the last change to the certificate and the monitor. A check simulates data whose truth is known and its draws sit inside the run, so its rows carry the settings of the run and no seed. The three runs repeat the runs of record before them, `e10` `20261013-223243_8502bf0` and `e14` `20261012-172545_72cac4a` in every line of their logs and `e20` `20261014-152612_622cb1e` in every file of its results. `e3_bounds_over_time.csv` follows the stream of seed 0 of `e21` and `e24` on the replay, offline FORGE at the typical composition under one sequence over the whole stream, the one stream whose trace those runs keep. `e9_ablations.csv` holds one run per variant of `e12` at seed 0, its base repeating seed 0 of `e12` above on the reference windows, and `e9_blaster_unseen.csv` the Blaster worm held out under three feature sets with the `e23` run of seed 0 as the base. `intro_for_by_prevalence.csv` reads no run: it is the FOR πm/(πm + (1 − π)b) of one threshold that dismisses b = 0.9 of the benign flows at miss rates m of 0.1%, 1% and 5%, with the prevalence where each line crosses ε = 10⁻³.
