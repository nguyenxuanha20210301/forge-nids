Each file here is written by `analysis/paper_results.py` from the pooled tables of the experiments over seeds and the run folders behind them. The tables under `tables/` are written first and every figure under `figures/` is drawn from them, so `python analysis/paper_results.py` reproduces the folder from the pulled runs, and `tables/settings.csv` holds the ε, δ, π_max and π_cal of every experiment with its first and last run id. The runs of `e21`, `e23` and `e24` hold two streams, the simulated one and the replay of the real order, in a `stream` column of their tables; the paper's figures and the E7 monitoring stage read the replay, the simulated stream stays beside it as the check. The runs of `e11`, `e12` and `e13` hold LTT at an estimated prevalence in a plug in and an upper bound variant, `ltt_plugin` and `ltt_upper`, beside the other methods. UGR16 runs at ε = 0.005 with π_max = 0.1, its operating target, and its certificate starts at half of the benign flows, the share start, where AnoShift and LITNET keep the feasible start; the fixed threshold and conformal FNR of `e13` start at the same share, where the certificate and LTT start, and the recalibration of `e24` starts from that fixed threshold. `e13_ugr16_floor` is `e13` at the paper's ε = 0.001, where the certificate refuses most calibration sets; its rows join the E1 tables and the settings with their ε, and the E1 figure keeps the operating target. `tables/settings.csv` carries the settings of every experiment.

Every pooled share of the E3, E4 and E6 tables is pooled over its own units, the draws, and the draws with an alarm for the share of alarms above the bound of Theorem 3; a seed without the share adds nothing, and a share no seed has stays empty. The tables over seeds read the run folders behind the pooled tables, every seed judging its methods and arms on the same calibration draws and windows, so a difference between two methods or two arms is paired within a seed and the seeds are independent. A difference comes with its median, its 5% to 95% range and its extremes over seeds, the seeds above, at and below zero, and the exact two-sided sign test, with the p values of a table adjusted together by Holm (`_p_holm`). The pooled shares of `e1_for_by_prevalence.csv` and `e3_by_policy.csv` carry two-sided 95% Clopper-Pearson intervals (`_ci_low`, `_ci_high`) taking the draws of every seed as the units, the sequences of every draw for the share of sequences leaving the bounds and the alarms for the share of alarms above the bound; the windows of one draw share its calibration set, so the E1 intervals count draws rather than draw and window pairs. `e1_start_rules.csv` redraws the calibration sets of every `e13` run with the numpy of the machine that writes it, the runs' own draws where it matches Colab's version and draws of the same law elsewhere.
